Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 11, 2026

Simple Summary: Mini Coders for Kids does NOT collect, store, or share any personal information. The app is designed to be a safe offline environment for children.

1. Introduction

Mini Coders for Kids ("the App") is committed to protecting the privacy of our users, especially children. This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding information collection and usage.

2. Data Collection

We do not collect any personal data. Specifically:

  • We do not collect names, email addresses, or phone numbers.
  • We do not collect location data.
  • We do not collect device identifiers (UDID/IDFA).
  • We do not use any third-party analytics or tracking software.

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

The App is designed for children under the age of 13. We strictly adhere to the Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Since we do not collect any personal information, we do not share any data with third parties.

4. App Progress Storage

Level progress and avatar selections are stored locally on your device. This data is not uploaded to any server and stays on your phone/tablet.

5. Advertisements

The App contains no advertisements and no hidden tracking mechanisms.

6. External Links

There are no external links to websites or social media within the gameplay area to ensure a safe experience for children.
